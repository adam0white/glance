# Playtest: Jade, 2026-10-19

Source-unread playtest of the live build (commit 4e2f35a, HF 2.7.0) on a 360x780 mobile context (headless Chromium, Playwright). Persona: Jade, 19, plays on the bus in short bursts, impatient. Model: Haiku (the build used Opus). Her words are recorded verbatim below.

## What changed because of it

Applied in 8e870e7 and the commit after it:
- Fast speed dropped from 3.5 to 2.4 sim-minutes a second, and an open sheet slows the clock to 0.35, so a choice no longer runs away while she reads it.
- A short tap on Warn Mill Lane now says "keep your finger on it…" instead of doing nothing.
- The log no longer slides under an open sheet at 360x780; the sheet keeps its height and the log fades at the top.
- New mid-night lines from Dai, Callum and Nerys fill the quiet stretches she called dead time.

## Her report

**1. First 60 seconds**

Okay so it's a river, a dam, a guy with a torch. Got it. The "Every figure on your board is what he saw" line made me think the numbers might lie, which was kind of interesting. I hit "Take the night desk" and it's a bit of a wall of text. I didn't read the paragraphs, I just tapped the gauges. The speed buttons were top right, I picked the fastest one straight away. Then the clock went 22:02, 22:23, 22:52 in like three seconds and nothing on the river moved. That's when I almost left.

**2. People or dice?**

Dai felt like a person, mostly. Lines that worked for me:
- "Easy. I'll have plenty for the next round." (when I asked for the gate)
- "Not that — I'll read the level." (when I sent him to the weir. He said no. Good.)
- "I'll do it. Don't ask me for much after."
- "(a long yawn) three-nine, near enough." (this one showed up a lot, it started to feel like a tic)
- "Ground's full. Whatever falls now comes straight down."

Nerys at the Bridge Inn felt like a real person too: "Ring if you need eyes on the river, love." The pencilled note "Dai reads low after three. —M." was the best bit of the whole thing for me. It made me think I could trust him less and then check him.

Wrong feeling: the river sat at 4.00 m for ages while it was raining, then jumped. It felt like the game was waiting for me to look.

**3. The sharpest choice you made, and what came of it**

On Tuesday I sent Dai to the weir. He refused. So I rang Nerys and held "Warn Mill Lane" (the warden knocked doors). Then the lane was fine, the warning was "for nothing," and the game said "The lane will remember." I caught 2 of 5 of Dai's readings. Honestly I'd have made the same call on Monday if I'd known to hold things.

**4. Bugs and layout problems**

- Monday 22:02 to 23:56: at the fastest speed, one tap on a gate turn meant 20 to 40 game minutes passed before I could look. Nothing visible changed in the meantime.
- Monday, first load: I did a sequence of taps and ended up on the framework's home page ("human framework", "Play Twice at the Well"). I can't tell which tap sent me there. It happened once.
- Monday, after I quit and came back: the title only showed "Take the night desk" and "Load a playtest run". I saw no "Back to the desk" option, so I started a new night.
- Tuesday 22:06, weir gauge: the label "to the weir" sits on top of the dashed river line and the rain, so it's hard to read.
- Gate panel: the six turn buttons go below the bottom of the screen at 360x780. The "shut / 2 / 3" row is fine but the bottom row is partly cut off, so I had to scroll to find them.
- Tuesday 22:37, Bridge Inn: a plain tap on "Warn Mill Lane" did nothing. The small grey text said "hold", but nothing told me that when I tapped. It cost me a minute.
- The gate and level readings dim to "Dai 22:00" and sit there. I couldn't tell if the number was still true.
- On the debrief page the chart is the only part I actually looked at. The text under it I skimmed.

**5. Your demands, ranked**

1. Give me something to do every ten seconds of game time, or let the clock stop when there's a choice waiting. At the fastest speed I was waiting, not playing.
2. Put the hold hint on the button itself, or make a tap do something visible.
3. Keep the turn buttons above the bottom of the screen at 360x780.
4. Show the save state on the title page clearly. Either "Back to the desk" or nothing. Don't hide it.
5. Fix the "to the weir" label so it doesn't sit on the river line.

**6. Verdict and score**

The people are the good part, and Nerys and the pencilled note hooked me, but at the fastest speed the game moves faster than I can react, so I mostly watched it happen. 5/10.
