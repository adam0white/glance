# Playtest: Tomás, 2026-10-19

Source-unread playtest of the live build (commit 4e2f35a, HF 2.7.0) on a 360x780 mobile context (headless Chromium, Playwright). Persona: Tomás, 44, a night-shift nurse who plays for dread and stakes (Papers, Please; This War of Mine). Model: Opus, run as a separate agent that had not read the source. His words are recorded verbatim below.

## What changed because of it

Applied in the commit after 8e870e7:
- The "after I open the gate" and "rather check the gate" loops are fixed (the gate offer exists only when a gate order is pending).
- Report times no longer drift before the shift: the stamp is clamped to the watch start and to 30 minutes before the report.
- Nerys's goodwill recovers between nights; when her phone rings out the log says why, and her late answers vary with how often she has been rung.
- Dai's and Callum's dawn lines vary by night and seed, and refer to an overtop when there was one.
- The dawn's explanation fits the direction of the error (Callum reads high when rushing).
- The header reads "Monday night" past midnight.
- Dozes read "fell asleep on watch" in the dawn, separate from sleep at the stove.
  Deferred (docs/design.md): keep both readings when keepers disagree, flag a doubted reading, and carry water and tiredness between nights.

## His report

## 1. First 60 seconds (what you understood, what confused you)

I liked the setup straight away. It's a night desk, one dam, one keeper, rain coming, and the line "Every figure on your board is what he saw." I understood within a minute that the numbers come from a tired man, not from the water. The drawing is clean: the dam, the crest and lip marks, the '79 flood mark, Dai's little hut. The speed buttons are clear.

What confused me:
- I didn't know where "lip" or "crest" sat in metres. I didn't know what the town downstream could take until Nerys told me on night 2: "Mill Lane gets it at five."
- The clock read "Monday 00:26", "Monday 04:02". Past midnight it should say Tuesday, or the header should show the night as Monday night.
- Each debrief ends with "You caught none of them." I never learned how you catch a bad reading. I compared readings against the lightning flashes and against the second keeper, and I acted on it. Nothing in the game let me flag a reading or tell anyone, so the line reads as a scold for a skill the game never showed me.

## 2. People or dice?

Mostly people, and that's why I kept playing.

Lines that landed:
- "Easy. I'll have plenty for the next round." Hours later the same gate request got "I'll do it. Don't ask me for much after." I watched him get tired without seeing a single bar.
- Dai on the kid, the first night Callum came: "He'll learn." The board had said "Dai has not said a word about him." After I sent him to sleep, Dai got up and went to the staff gauge himself to check Callum's number. I've worked with that man.
- Callum: "Sorry, sir. The baby's teething. I'm fine." And in the debrief: "Callum hurt his shoulder on the wheel. He didn't say." That's exactly how people hide things on a ward.
- "You sent me to sleep on watch. Thirty years nobody did that." Then, after I held the button to make him sleep: "You leaned on me once. Once is enough." I felt that.
- The warden, after my false alarm on night 2: "He said they'd heard that one before, but he would knock." The game remembered my mistake and didn't preach about it. Then Mill Lane flooded before anyone knocked. That was the best moment of the week.
- Lines like "Ground's full. Whatever falls now comes straight down." and "There's a tree down on the screen. She's choking." are dread done right.
- Seeing Dai's stick figure asleep at the staff gauge, then later asleep on the crest with a little "z", made my stomach drop.

Lines that felt like dice:
- Dai's two debrief lines were word for word the same on all four nights: "I didn't keep my watch. Next time I'll be ready when it comes." From night 3 on, Callum got a fixed pair too.
- Nerys opened every call with "Mm? What time is it… Hang on, I'll look.", even when I'd rung her 25 minutes earlier. On night 2 she gave the same answer twice word for word ("Fourth step. That's high. Mill Lane gets it at five.").
- From Wednesday on, Nerys never answered again ("The phone at the Bridge Inn rings out", six times over two nights). If she's annoyed or asleep, I want one line saying so. As it is, it feels broken.
- Every error in the debrief has the same explanation: "He was short of sleep, and further behind than he felt." That doesn't fit Callum, who reads _high_.
- Some readings came out garbled, such as "nought point 17 nought" for a weir board that showed 1.70 m.

## 3. The sharpest choice you made, and what came of it

On night 2, at about 03:19, Nerys said the river was at the fourth step and Mill Lane goes at five. Dai's last reading was 4.7, and the margin note says "Dai reads low after three." Then Dai went "not answering" and I saw him asleep on the dam face. I held "Warn Mill Lane" and the warden knocked doors at 03:58. The river never reached Mill Lane: "The warning was for nothing. The lane will remember."

It did remember. On Thursday a tree choked the gate, the level went well over the lip, and I warned at 03:29. The warden said they'd heard that one before, and "Mill Lane flooded before anyone knocked." I made the right call for the wrong night and paid for it two nights later. That's the game I want to play.

## 4. Bugs and layout problems

**"After I open the gate" loop.** It happened on Tuesday around 22:40, and again on Thursday at 22:13 and 23:07. I asked a keeper to "Get your head down" and he answered "after I open the gate". He walked to the wheel and reported "Gate's on one turn" (or "three turns") twice in a row, with no change. Then the button said "after I sit by the stove", while he was already by the stove. Sitting took him back to "after I open the gate". The only way out was holding the button to insist, which costs his goodwill. On Thursday, after 03:51, Callum also stuck on "rather check the gate": he answered "Not that — I'll check the gate" and reported "six turns" again and again. He wouldn't read the level.

**Report times shifted about 30 minutes in the board and debrief.**
- Monday: the log said "00:17 Dai four-two" but the card said "Dai 23:47".
- Tuesday: the card said "Dai 02:59" for a reading I asked for at 03:18.
- Thursday: the debrief lists "21:48 Dai said four-nought", before the shift started at 22:00.
- The level panel's own "Last: Dai, 69 minutes ago" matched the log, not the card.

**Sleep record disagrees with what I saw.** I ordered sleep at 02:48 Monday, 00:12 Tuesday, 23:51 Wednesday and 23:33 Thursday, and the screen said "asleep, as you said". Most of those sleeps never appear in the debrief's "slept by the stove" list. Tuesday at 03:59 and Thursday at 04:11, I saw Dai drawn asleep on the dam, but the debrief says "slept by the stove". Also on Thursday at 04:11, Callum was drawn outside the hut while his status said "by the stove".

**Overlapping labels on the dam drawing:**
- "lightning 03:34" sits on top of "lip" (Monday 04:02).
- "lightning 01:34" runs into the wall (Tuesday).
- "radio: static" covers Dai's figure on the hut (Thursday 02:25).
- At high water, "5.4 m · 02:43 · Dai" collides with "crest" and "to the weir", and Dai's name sits on the '79 mark (Thursday 04:11).

**Panels at 360×780:**
- The gate panel's second row (4/5/6) is cut off at the bottom edge (Monday 02:18).
- An open panel pushes the log up, and new log lines disappear behind it.
- "not answering" wraps to two lines on Dai's card when there are three cards.

**Board overwrites readings.** A second reading replaces the first, so Callum's 4.3 vanished when Dai's 3.8 came in. When two men disagree, I want to see both.

**Fresh start each night.** Wednesday ended with water climbing towards the lip and the gate on four. Thursday opened with "Level's four-one, gate's on one turn. Still raining.", almost word for word Tuesday's opening.

## 5. Your demands, ranked

1. Fix the "after I open the gate" loop. It's the one thing that made me feel I was fighting the code, not the men.
2. Let me catch errors, or drop "You caught none of them." For example, let me mark a reading as doubted, or send a second keeper to check it, and have the debrief score that.
3. Give Nerys a life. One line when she stops answering (asleep, fed up, the phone line down), and different words on each call.
4. Make the debrief talk to me. Use different words each night, and an explanation per keeper ("reads high when he's rushing" for Callum). Have Dai refer to what actually happened that night.
5. Keep both readings on the board when they disagree. Fix the 30-minute time shift.
6. Carry the water and the men's tiredness from one night to the next. You already carry the warden's grudge, so carry the rest.
7. Tidy the labels and the panel heights at phone size.

## 6. Verdict

The dread is real and the men have lives. One looping bug and a debrief on repeat keep it from being the Papers, Please of dams. **7/10**
